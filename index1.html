While you wait for us

A few minutes of action before the crew arrives can limit the damage. Only do these if it’s safe.

Do

  • Stop the sourceShut off the water at the valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Turn off power to wet areasUse the breaker panel if water is near outlets or cords.
  • Lift small itemsMove furniture, rugs and electronics out of the water.
  • Take photosCapture the damage before anything moves, for your insurer.

Don’t

  • Don’t walk through standing water near electricityWait until power is off.
  • Don’t use a household vacuumIt’s not made for water and can be dangerous.
  • Don’t seal the room upOpen windows and run fans to keep air moving.
  • Don’t wait to see if it driesMold can start growing within 48 hours.

Questions we hear a lot

How long does drying take?

Most jobs take three to five days, depending on how much water there was, what it soaked into and the weather. We tell you what to expect after the first measurements.

Can my carpet be saved?

Often, yes, especially when clean water was involved and we reach it within a day or two. If carpet or pad can’t be saved, we tell you before removing anything.

Will insurance cover it?

Many policies cover sudden water damage, such as a burst pipe. We document everything with photos and daily readings and can work directly with your adjuster. Coverage depends on your policy.

Do you offer emergency water damage services in Northwest Indiana?

Yes. Swift Dry takes emergency calls around the clock and serves Northwest Indiana. Call (219) 713-9252 and we will tell you how soon a crew can reach you.

What should I do if my basement floods?

If it is safe, shut off the water source and turn off power to the area at the breaker panel. Stay out of standing water near outlets or cords, move belongings to higher ground, take photos for your insurer, and call us right away. Avoid using a household vacuum on water.

How much does water damage restoration cost?

The cost depends on how large the affected area is, what kind of water it was, which materials got wet and how many days of drying equipment are needed. We give a free estimate before any work begins, so you know what to expect.

How can I tell if water is hiding behind my walls or under my floors?

Common signs include a musty smell, peeling or bubbling paint, stained ceilings, warped or spongy floors and carpet that stays damp. We use moisture meters and thermal cameras to find water you cannot see.

Can I dry a wet carpet myself?

A small spill on the surface, yes. But water usually soaks into the pad and subfloor, where household fans and towels can’t reach it. If the wet area is large or has been wet for more than a day, professional drying lowers the risk of mold and odors.

How quickly can mold grow after water damage?

Mold can begin to grow within 24 to 48 hours in damp materials. That is why fast extraction and thorough drying matter, even when the water looks like it has gone.

Do you remove mold?

Yes. If mold has already started to grow, we contain the affected area, remove materials that can’t be saved and clean surrounding surfaces. We also dry the space completely so the moisture that fed the mold is gone.

Do you dry commercial properties?

Yes. We dry offices, shops and rental units, and we schedule work around your business hours to keep downtime low.

Is the equipment loud?

Air movers and dehumidifiers make noticeable noise, similar to a box fan. We position them to be as unobtrusive as the job allows.

Do you handle sewage or floodwater?

Contaminated water needs different safety steps and often full removal of porous materials. Call us and we’ll explain what’s possible for your situation.
